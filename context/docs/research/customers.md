## Case studies (results as written)

**Superbox, Jewels Jungle** (https://almedia.co/resource-hub/case-studies/superbox-jewels-jungle)
- "D30 ROAS: 1.5–2× higher compared to non-rewarded UA channels"
- "Retention (D30): 3× higher than other rewarded UA channels (2% → 6%)"
- "Sustainable growth: 4x daily spend growth achieved"
- Jade Baeck, Senior Manager at SUPERBOX: "Almedia delivered nearly 3x higher D30 retention compared to other rewarded channels: all while maintaining strong ROAS."

**Candivore, Match Masters** (https://almedia.co/resource-hub/case-studies/candivore-match-masters)
- "D90 ROAS: Increased by 70% between February and April"
- "D7 ROAS: Increased by 40%, to 2x the D7 goal"; "D30 ROAS: Increased by 65%, to 2x the D30 goal"; "Retention (D60): Increased by 50%"; "Ad Spend: Successfully scaled by 25% without compromising performance"; "Reward Window Extended: From 50 to 90 days"
- Adi Tubul, User Acquisition Manager at Candivore: "Almedia helped us scale high-quality U.S. users across iOS and Android, driving a 120% uplift in Day 7 retention and ROAS…" (https://almedia.co/contact)

**TREEPLLA, Office Cat** (https://almedia.co/resource-hub/case-studies/treeplla-office-cat)
- "D30 ROAS: Surpassed competitor benchmarks by 2x, and Treeplla's own targets by 50%"; "Ad Spend: Successfully scaled daily spend by 550% while maintaining strong ROAS"; "Market Expansion: Successful launch in four new high-value markets (DE, GB, CA, FR)"
- San Heo, CEO, Treeplla: "We came into this partnership with aggressive growth goals, and Freecash helped us blow past every one of them."
- Dukryul Kim, UA Manager, Treeplla: "This was more than a reactivation, it was a relaunch powered by insight."

**Potato Play** (https://almedia.co/resource-hub/case-studies/potato-play)
- "+20% Install Growth"; "+40% Spend Growth"; "+26% D30 ARPU Growth"; "3× Geo Expansion: Scaled from 5 to 15 markets, including EU and Tier-2 regions"
- Saras Panigrahi, Marketing Director at Potato Play: "We required UA partners who can perform at high spend levels and still hit key metrics like retention…"

**Ovivo (Link)** (https://almedia.co/resource-hub/case-studies/ovivo-link)
- "15% install-to-link conversion"; "+28% D21 retention uplift"; "66,000 links across 24 titles"; "an estimated 16.8% LTV uplift across exposed users"
- Dima Gorodnev, COO, Ovivo: "Partnering with Freecash has been a great fit for our games and community."

**Gryphon Games (Link)** (https://almedia.co/resource-hub/case-studies/gryphon-games-link)
- "18.6% higher total publisher revenue per user, including all in-game revenue and Link CPA payments."; "5.2% higher Day 7 retention."
- Dylan Aguiar, Co-Founder at Gryphon Games: "For any studio with players to monetise, Link is an absolute essential."
