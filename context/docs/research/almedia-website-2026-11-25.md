## Products

- **Freecash**: "Almedia's flagship rewarded engagement platform" (https://almedia.co/advertise/freecash/games). Sold to game studios (Freecash for Games) and brands (Freecash for Brands, https://almedia.co/advertise/freecash/brands).
- **Link**: "an integrated in-game widget that lets users register on our app Freecash" (https://almedia.co/monetize/link/games); "an integrated in-app widget" (https://almedia.co/monetize/link/apps). "We pay CPA per link."
- **MaxROAS**: "our proprietary algorithm" (https://almedia.co/advertise/freecash/brands)
