## 2026 predictions (https://almedia.co/insights/blog/2026-in-rewarded-ua-almedia-predictions, 2025-12-17)

- "Once met with skepticism, 2025 saw rewarded UA prove its value"
- "advertisers are measuring true success not by the volume of installs, but on post-install value"
- "Many advertisers already aim to reach 100% ROAS before the rewarded window ends" / "This is most common with smaller studios"
- Prediction headings: "D360 Will be a Meaningful Metric"; "The Non-Gaming Floodgates Will Open"; "Devs Who Don't Design Around Rewarded UA Will be Left Behind"; "Rewarded's Advantage Will Grow Even Further Thanks to Data Constraints"
- Non-gaming verticals named: retail, finance, education, iGaming, healthcare/fitness. "We'll probably see the swiftest growth in adoption from fintech"
- "We compared rewarded users from Almedia to Google users for a top mobile game": D180 ROAS 104.43% vs 46.38%; D180 ARPU $9.91 vs $3.84 (game unnamed)
- Expects consolidation into "1-2 clear leaders"
- Product quality framed as "Strong product quality to sustain UA success"
