# Almedia: company

Raw research, not conclusions. All sources checked 2026-11-25 unless noted. Quotes are exact as fetched (long quotes may be truncated, marked "…"). Anything marked INFERRED is my reading, not a stated fact. treg was not installed, so no paid data sources were used.

## What they sell

- Almedia is "Berlin-based Almedia powers user acquisition through its flagship platform Freecash, engaging over 95 million users with data-driven rewards across games, apps, and surveys." (https://almedia.co/press)
- Business model: "Advertisers pay us when a user takes a real action, like playing for a set amount of time or making a purchase." (https://almedia.co/about)
- Three-step flow: "User plays, tests or shops in Freecash" / "Advertiser pays us for the in-app action" / "User earns a real reward" (https://almedia.co/about)
- Founder on revenue: "Our revenue comes from advertisers, primarily mobile game publishers and retailers." (Holländer statement, quoted in https://pocketgamer.biz/almedia-responds-after-freecash-removal-from-app-store-and-google-play/, published 2026-04-17)
- 2024 description: Almedia "refers to itself as a gamified promotion platform that aims to provide benefits to both advertisers and consumers." (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-almedia-is-riding-the-rewarded-play-trend, published 2024-11-12)

## Pricing model

- Advertisers (Freecash): pay per user action. "Advertisers pay us when a user takes a real action…" (https://almedia.co/about). The Superbox case study says it was "a CPI-based rewarded user acquisition campaign" (https://almedia.co/resource-hub/case-studies/superbox-jewels-jungle).
- Publishers (Link): Almedia pays them. "We pay CPA per link" and "Paid per verified link, with transparent reporting." "Receive monthly payment for all users who connect to Freecash from your game" (https://almedia.co/monetize/link/games)
- Example in image alt text only (not visible copy): "$5.23 CPA" (https://almedia.co/monetize/link/apps)
- Price levels, minimum spend, CPE, rev share: Not found.

## Reputation and risk events

- 2026-04-13: Apple removed Freecash from the App Store; Google Play followed about 2026-04-15. Google Play reinstated 2026-05-07, App Store 2026-06-17. (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/almedias-freecash-returns-to-apple-app-store-following-removal/, 2026-06-17)
- TechCrunch headline: "How the rewards app Freecash scammed its way to the top of the app stores" and called it "a data harvesting app" (https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/14/how-the-rewards-app-freecash-scammed-its-way-to-the-top-of-the-app-stores/, 2026-04-14). Apple cited misleading marketing, per TechCrunch.
- Almedia response: removal followed "inaccurate and misleading claims about our business" and "We are not perfect. We are a fast-growing company, and fast growth comes with mistakes that need correcting." (Holländer, via https://pocketgamer.biz/almedia-responds-after-freecash-removal-from-app-store-and-google-play/). Almedia acknowledged "misleading affiliate ads earlier this year that have since been removed" (same source, paraphrase).
- Earlier removals: Google Play in Jan 2024 and App Store in June 2024, per Appfigures via TechCrunch (above).
- Trustpilot (seen 2026-11-25): TrustScore 4.6, 341,367 reviews; label "This company may be associated with high-risk investments." (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/freecash.com)
- BBB: 1.14 of 5 from 90 reviews, not accredited, "Pattern of Complaints" alert, per Finder as of 2026-07-13 (https://www.finder.com/make-money-online/freecash-review). BBB page itself: Not fetched.
