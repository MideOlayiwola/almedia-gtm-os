## Rewarded UA moving to core spend (mostly vendor sources)

- "Rewarded UA has moved from a capped testing phase to core spend." (Almedia-authored post on Singular: https://www.singular.net/blog/rewarded-user-acquisition/, 2026-01-21)
- "Categories with tight ad restrictions like fintech and iGaming will shift budget into rewarded" (same source, Almedia prediction)
- Almedia survey of 502 US and UK mobile game developers (Atomik Research, fieldwork 2024-11-25 to 2024-12-04): 77% have run reward-based UA; 82% say it outperforms traditional UA; 59% plan to increase spend in 2025. (https://www.gamespress.com/82-OF-MOBILE-GAME-DEVELOPERS-SAY-REWARD-BASED-UA-OUTPERFORMS-TRADITION, 2025-01-20)
- "61% of mobile game developers plan to increase their rewarded UA budget in 2026" (https://almedia.co/advertise/freecash/games; no source given on page)
- Holländer: "Rewarded user acquisition has existed for 15 years, but it changed in the way it works." (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-almedia-is-riding-the-rewarded-play-trend, 2024-11-12)
- AppsFlyer index (2024): "16 (20%)" of "~79 ad providers" offer incentivized traffic (https://morerewarding.substack.com/p/appsflyer-performance-index-17-top, 2024-10-17)

## Apple / app store rules (US)

- April 2025 contempt ruling (Epic v. Apple): Apple "cannot charge fees on external purchases"; developers "can now place a simple, frictionless web shop link inside their game". Lower-fee competitors "will inevitably have higher lifetime net revenues, allowing them to bid more aggressively." (https://naavik.co/digest/apple-removes-app-store-payment-restrictions-in-the-us/, dated 2025-05-11)
- 2025-12-11 9th Circuit: "an all-out ban on commissions isn't appropriate", but "Today's decision doesn't yet allow Apple to continue charging a commission on linked-out purchases yet." A fair rate is still to be set. (https://9to5mac.com/2025/12/11/apple-defeats-ban-on-charging-commission-on-linked-out-purchases-from-ios-apps/)
- INFERRED: web shops raise margin per payer, which can raise what studios can afford to bid per user. Not stated by any source in relation to rewarded UA.
- Rewarded apps and Apple: "Rewarded apps have had a complicated relationship with Apple." (Holländer, via https://pocketgamer.biz/almedia-responds-after-freecash-removal-from-app-store-and-google-play/, 2026-04-17). Freecash was removed from the App Store 2026-04-13 and restored 2026-06-17 (see company.md).
- ppc.land refers to an Apple guideline change Almedia relied on (section 3.2.2(x)), dated both "mid-2025" and 2025-11-13 in different articles. Not verified.

## EU regulation

- Digital Fairness Act proposal published 2026-11-11 (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/finnish-and-german-games-associations-warn-eu-digital-fairness-act-could-hurt-industry/, 2026-11-13). Covers in-game purchases, virtual currencies, pay-to-win and age ratings. Fines "up to 6% of global annual turnover". German games association: in-game purchases generated €4.1bn in Germany in 2025.
- Pinsent Masons: the DFA would "replace the EU's Unfair Commercial Practices Directive (UCPD)"; Art. 14 paid virtual currencies "disabled by default" for children; Art. 15 paid variable-reward systems (loot boxes); Art. 16 pay-to-win for children (https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/digital-fairness-act-proposal-puts-video-game-monetisation-models-in-focus, 2026-11-19; partly from a fetch summary, not verbatim)
- Loyalty-programme points excluded from the virtual currency definition, per search summary of law-firm commentary (not verified on page).
- Rewarded ads or rewards apps: not mentioned in either DFA source.
- CPC Network principles on in-game virtual currencies (March 2025): enforcement position, not law (search summary; https://techinsights.linklaters.com/post/102k6t4/game-changer-eu-introduces-consumer-protection-guidance-for-in-game-virtual-curr, not fetched)
- INFERRED: if paid currencies and loot boxes face limits, studios may need other monetization and retention levers. No source links this to rewarded UA.

## Data privacy and platform scrutiny

- Malwarebytes (Jan 2026, cited by TechCrunch) said Freecash "may collect information about users' race, religion, sex life, sexual orientation, health…" (https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/14/how-the-rewards-app-freecash-scammed-its-way-to-the-top-of-the-app-stores/). Almedia says it "does not sell user data" and is GDPR compliant (PocketGamer, 2026-04-17, paraphrase).
- TikTok pulled some Freecash ads for financial misrepresentation (TechCrunch, above).
