## Stated by Almedia as triggers

- Soft and global launch: "63% of mobile game developers include rewarded at soft and global launch." (https://almedia.co/advertise/freecash/brands; no source given)
- Contact page reasons: "Scale UA, launch in new markets, or start monetizing your game." (https://almedia.co/)
- Short-term ROAS focus as a mistake: Jan Sommerfeld "on why an over-reliance on short-term ROAS is the mobile sector's most common mistake" (PocketGamer, 2026-01-07, headline listed on https://almedia.co/press; article not fetched)
- Ad-restricted categories: "Categories with tight ad restrictions like fintech and iGaming will shift budget into rewarded" (https://www.singular.net/blog/rewarded-user-acquisition/, 2026-01-21, Almedia-authored)

## Observable signals to look for in a prospect (INFERRED, from the evidence above)

- Casual, puzzle, merge or word game studio running paid UA (matches every named customer's genre)
- Hiring UA managers or announcing a new market launch, especially US, DE, GB, CA, FR (the top payout markets and TREEPLLA's launch markets)
- Soft launch or global launch announced
- Large catalogue of live titles with existing players (Link fit)
- Using other rewarded networks already (Superbox comparison was against "other rewarded UA channels")
- Retailer or fintech app with ad restrictions (brands product, cashback via zave.it)
