## Observations (INFERRED)

- Studios use several rewarded networks at once (SpringComes: Almedia Link, adjoe, Gamelight; TREEPLLA: Almedia, Mistplay; Unico Studio: Gamelight, Almedia Summit).
- Mistplay and adjoe both report results "over other rewarded partners", the same kind of comparison Almedia uses. This weakens "head to head" as a sole differentiator; see `context/positioning/positioning.md`.
