## Positioning statement

For mobile game studios already buying UA who need players that still pay back at D30 and beyond, Almedia is the rewarded user acquisition platform built on Freecash, its own rewards app with 95M+ users. Its results are measured against the other rewarded channels studios already use: Superbox saw 3x higher D30 retention than its other rewarded sources, and TREEPLLA beat competitor D30 ROAS benchmarks by 2x. Studios with live players can add Link, which pays them a CPA for every player who links to Freecash; Gryphon Games saw 18.6% higher total revenue per user. [CO], [CU]
