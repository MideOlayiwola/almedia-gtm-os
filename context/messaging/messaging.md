# Almedia messaging

Built only from `context/docs/research/`. Source key: [CO] company.md, [CU] customers.md, [VO] voice.md, [LP] leadership-posts.md, [MK] market.md, [CP] competitors.md, [RN] rival-network-clients.md. Exact phrasings and approval status live in `context/messaging/facts.md`.

## Core message

Rewarded users who keep paying back long after day 7, and a CPA for every player you already have who links to Freecash. [CU], [CO]

Why these two lead: head-to-head proof is no longer unique (Mistplay also reports results "over other rewarded partners" [RN]). Long-window value (D60 to D90 proof) and Link's CPA-per-linked-player model are what no competitor in the research claims the same way [RN], [CP]. Caveat: Persona.ly says a client's ROAS "continued growing past D30" [RN], so lead with Almedia's specific D60 and D90 numbers, not the general idea.

Supporting credibility (after a pillar, never as one): 95M+ Freecash users; FT1000 #3 in Europe, #1 in Advertising; AppsFlyer 2026 #1 rewarded on iOS (Almedia's claim, verify before use). [CO]

## Words and angles to avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| "scam", "data", "data broker", "bots" framing, or defending against them unprompted | Ties to 2026 TechCrunch and Malwarebytes coverage [CO], [MK] |
| "Highest Paying Rewards App" and other consumer cash language | Consumer copy, not B2B; Trustpilot "high-risk investments" label [VO], [CO] |
| Guarantees of ROAS or installs | All results are case-specific, Almedia-published [CU] |
| "Not installs" / "pay only for engagement" | Contradicted: Superbox ran "a CPI-based rewarded user acquisition campaign" [CO] |
| Leading with "we own our inventory", "biggest audience" or "smart algorithm" | Competitors claim the same (Mistplay, exmox, adjoe, Gamelight, Buzzvil) [CP] |
| Unsourced survey stats (61%, 63%) | No source given [MK] |
| Opening with "we beat other rewarded networks" or "only" claims | Mistplay makes the same head-to-head claim [RN] |
| D180 or D360 numbers in copy | Only one unnamed-game comparison (D180) and a prediction (D360) [LP] |
| Link conversion or Ovivo LTV without "projected" / "estimated" | That is how Almedia states them [CU], [CO] |
| Payout totals other than the latest dated figure | $300M, $400M and $500M appear at different dates [CO] |
| Em dashes, plus the global list in `knowledge-base/playbooks/copy-bans.yaml` | CLAUDE.md rule 3 |
